Our Story

A quiet corner of Italy, poured daily in Bahria Town.

Meseta began with a simple idea — that great coffee deserves a room that feels like a hug. Warm brick, natural wood, soft amber light; a place where the espresso is pulled with intent and nobody rushes you out of your seat.
